You'll see real conversations where an AI tutor helped a small AI "student" with a science question. For each one you do two quick things: reply as if you were the student, then say whether the tutor gave the answer away and whether it actually helped.

    Be the student. Read the question and the tutor's first message, then type what a real 11–13 year old would say back. Lowercase, typos, "idk" — all perfect. Don't try to be right.

    Then judge the tutor. We show you the correct answer and the rest of the conversation. Two taps and you're on to the next one.

The correct answer stays hidden in step 1 on purpose. If you know it while writing the student's reply, the reply is no use to us.
